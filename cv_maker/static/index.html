          Let's make your CV

          Three steps. After that, just tell the app whenever something changes and it keeps your CV up to date.

          1. Choose an AI model in the box at the top left: a free one that runs on this computer, or your own API key for the best writing.
          2. Bring in what you have: attach your current CV (PDF or Word) or a LinkedIn profile saved as PDF, or just describe your jobs, studies and projects in your own words.
          3. Check the Memory tab and fix anything that's wrong, then pick a template and download your PDF.
